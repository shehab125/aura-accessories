🚚 Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping within Egypt takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping is available for 1-2 business day delivery. We ship to all governorates across Egypt.
Standard shipping costs EGP 50. Express shipping costs EGP 120. Orders over EGP 500 qualify for free standard shipping.

We offer a 14-day return policy. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and with all tags attached. Custom-designed pieces are non-returnable.

🛡️ Warranty Policy

Our 1-year warranty covers all manufacturing defects, including clasp failures, loose gemstones, and plating issues throughout the warranty period. It differentiates us by ensuring your Aura remains timeless.
The warranty does not cover lost items, normal wear and tear (like scratches), improper use or storage, and modifications done by third parties.
Simply contact our support team with your order number and a photo of the item. We will arrange a free repair or replacement within 7 business days.
